OUR MISSION

More information
from every cell.

We believe spatial biology can provide deeper insights into health and disease when researchers can detect more molecular information with greater sensitivity and in increasingly complex systems.

OUR APPROACH

Oligo-fluorophore detection probes for next-generation spatial biology.

Toucan is focused on oligo-fluorophore technologies with differentiated spectral characteristics, high brightness, and flexible architectures for increasingly multiplexed single-cell measurements.
